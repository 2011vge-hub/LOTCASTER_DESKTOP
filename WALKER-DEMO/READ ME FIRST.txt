LOTCASTER — WALKER DEMO
=======================

This is the only folder you need during the Walker demonstration.

1. Double-click: 1 - START LOTCASTER.cmd
2. Sign in to LotCaster.
3. Confirm the LotCaster Facebook Helper reports version 1.8.0 or newer.
4. Click Refresh Inventory and wait for the completion message.
5. Open one vehicle and build its listing packet.
6. Review every price, field, and photo before any manual publication.

If something looks wrong:

1. Do not publish the listing.
2. Double-click: 2 - CHECK LOTCASTER.cmd
3. Save the exact message shown by LotCaster.

C:\Users\Gage\Documents\Codex\LotCaster

Older copies are backups. Do not launch them.
