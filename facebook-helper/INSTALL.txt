LOTCASTER EXPERIMENTAL LISTING HELPER 1.8.0

1. Open Edge and go to edge://extensions, or Chrome and go to chrome://extensions.
2. Turn on Developer mode in the upper-right corner.
3. Click Load unpacked.
4. Select this facebook-helper folder.
5. Refresh LotCaster.

After an update, close every open Facebook Marketplace tab, click Reload on the
extension card, refresh LotCaster, and verify that LotCaster reports the new
helper version. Existing Facebook tabs keep the old helper code until closed.
The helper can attempt a maximum of 8 authorized listing photos.

Version 1.8.0 loads AutoTrader dealership inventory in verified 25-vehicle
pages because AutoTrader now redirects the old 100-record address.

This helper is experimental and may assist with form fields, but never presses Publish.
After Facebook opens, choose listing type Car/Truck manually. LotCaster waits for
the vehicle form, lets Facebook finish VIN decoding, then corrects Year and Make
before filling dependent fields. It uses exact field labels and never interacts
with Listing location.
Verify every field and photo, then complete the final posting step manually.
