LotCaster training

Training & Help

Use LotCaster to prepare accurate listing packets and track dealership posting work. Final publishing always remains a manual user action.

Salesperson quick start

  1. Find an assigned or ready vehicle.
  2. Open its Listing Packet.
  3. Resolve any missing-field warnings.
  4. Copy the approved details and photos.
  5. Review the destination form and publish manually.
  6. Return to LotCaster and mark the vehicle posted.

Manager quick start

  1. Confirm the authorized Inventory Source.
  2. Import or refresh inventory.
  3. Review the import result and warnings.
  4. Spot-check pricing, mileage, categories, and photos.
  5. Export CSV for backup or review.

Daily posting checklist

  • Refresh inventory before preparing listings.
  • Verify price, mileage, VIN, stock number, and category.
  • Use clear photos that belong to the selected vehicle.
  • Review the dealership footer and listing description.
  • Mark completed work posted in LotCaster.

Listing best practices

  • Use the generated title and keep facts consistent.
  • Lead with the best exterior photo.
  • Avoid unsupported claims or outdated pricing.
  • Keep contact and availability language dealership-approved.

Compliance reminders

  • Verify every listing before publishing.
  • Use only inventory data and photos your dealership is authorized to use.
  • LotCaster prepares information; it does not publish listings or control social accounts.
  • The user manually completes the final posting step.

What not to do

  • Do not share Facebook passwords with LotCaster.
  • Do not automate buyer messages or account actions.
  • Do not publish without checking vehicle availability.
  • Do not reuse photos from a different vehicle.
